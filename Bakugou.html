Katsuki Bakugo

About

Katsuki Bakugo is a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School. His hero name is Great Explosion Murder God Dynamight. His Explosion Quirk allows him to create powerful blasts using the sweat from his palms. He is confident, competitive, strong-willed, and determined to become the number one hero. Although he can be aggressive, he works hard to improve his abilities and become a stronger hero.
